🩺 What To Do During An Emergency

1

Stay Calm

Try to remain calm and assess the situation.

2

Call For Help

Contact emergency services or ask someone nearby to call.

3

Follow Professional Instructions

Follow instructions provided by emergency responders.

4

Do Not Delay Medical Care

Seek professional medical attention as soon as possible.
